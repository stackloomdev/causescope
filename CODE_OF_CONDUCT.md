# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in CauseScope respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and kind.
- Assume good intent while addressing observable behavior and evidence.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's secrets or private source code.
- Accept feedback and focus on what improves the project for users.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately to `email@loupengju.cc`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
